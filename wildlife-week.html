Competitions & Performances

Festival Events & Competitions

14 high-energy challenges divided across morning competitive tracks and evening cultural stage events.

Field & Quest ₹50 / Person

Wildlife Treasure Hunt

Follow campus-wide clues covering animal behavior, ecosystems, and national parks to track down hidden locations. Each team receives a unique starting clue to test their wildlife knowledge and speed.

10:00 AM – 4:00 PM Register
Trivia & Sounds ₹50 / Person

The Ultimate Wildlife Quiz

Put your nature smarts to the test across intense rounds of visual identification, animal sound clips, and rapid-fire trivia. Conclude with a high-stakes Risk Round where harder questions yield higher points.

10:00 AM – 4:00 PM Register
Digital Media ₹50 / Person

Wildlife Reel Battle

Challengers have two hours to shoot and edit a creative 60-second video on topics like animal perspectives or conservation myths. Submissions are judged on scientific accuracy, storytelling, and impact, with top reels featured on social media.

10:00 AM – 4:00 PM Register
Policy & Debate ₹50 / Person

Wild Debate

Teams get just 10 minutes to prepare arguments on controversial topics like ecotourism, zoos, and predator reintroduction. Engage in fast-paced debates to defend or challenge real-world conservation strategies.

10:00 AM – 4:00 PM Register
Interactive Game ₹50 / Person

Wildlife Myth Busters

An interactive audience game where participants quickly vote TRUE or FALSE on common nature misconceptions like "bats are blind". Uncover the scientific truth behind everyday wildlife legends through rapid-fire explanations.

10:00 AM – 4:00 PM Register
Forensics & Mystery ₹50 / Person

Wildlife Detective Analyze

Camera-trap photos, footprint casts, witness statements, and map timelines to solve the mystery of a missing leopard. Teams have 60 minutes to piece together evidence, identify suspects, and construct the full case report.

10:00 AM – 4:00 PM Register
Art & Canvas ₹50 / Person

Wild Face Championship

Transform a partner's face into a wild creature or conservation theme using non-toxic paints. Artists present a 30-second storytelling pitch to explain the conservation concept behind their visual design.

10:00 AM – 4:00 PM Register
Creative Modeling ₹50 / Person

Wild A–Z: Alphabet of Conservation

Teams randomly draw a single letter from A to Z and build a functional, interactive model tied directly to that letter. Present your scientific creation to judges to show how original design connects to wildlife preservation.

10:00 AM – 4:00 PM Register
Eco-Artistry ₹50 / Person

Wild Rangoli: Nature's Canvas

Craft intricate eco-rangolis strictly out of fallen leaves, petals, seeds, and soil without using artificial colors or paints. Teams get 90 minutes to design an artistic piece that visualizes biodiversity or vanishing habitats.

10:00 AM – 4:00 PM Register
Sculpture & Upcycling ₹50 / Person

Wild Recycle: Animal from Trash

Transform discarded waste and scrap materials into a mini animal sculpture complete with its natural habitat. Work against a 60-minute clock to build creative art pieces that highlight human waste impact on ecosystems.

10:00 AM – 4:00 PM Register
Cultural Stage ₹100 / Person

Dance Competition

High-octane choreography celebrating animal locomotion, nature's rhythms, and dramatic folklore.

Participation Formats: Solo / Duo / Group (Max. 5 People).

Evening Grand Stage Register

Singing & Band Performance

Melodic vocal anthems and live acoustic or band tracks honoring the spirit of the jungle and ecological harmony.

Participation Formats: Solo / Duo / Band (Max. 5 People).

Evening Grand Stage Register
Cultural Stage ₹100 / Person

Poetry Slam

Spoken word and lyrical verses illuminating the beauty, resilience, and plight of endangered wildlife.

Participation Format: Solo recitation.

Evening Grand Stage Register

Wild Talent Showcase

Open-stage expression featuring mono-acting, instrumental solos, mimicry of animal calls, stand-up comedy, and theatrical acts.

Participation Format: Solo performance.
